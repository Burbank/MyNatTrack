# NAT HLA & Bordering FIRs – Named Five-Letter Waypoints (5LNCs) Sample

**Disclaimer**  
This is an extensive but **incomplete sample** compiled from public sources (FAA New York Oceanic pilot briefings, OpenNav, AirportGuide, ICAO NAT documentation, Portuguese AIP references, and related publications).  

It focuses on five-letter named waypoints in or bordering the NAT HLA, with emphasis on New York Oceanic East (KZWY) / WATRS and Santa Maria OAC (LPPO).  

**Do not use for navigation.** Always verify against current official AIPs, FAA NFDC data, Portuguese AIP, ICAO ICARD, or a licensed navigation database. Coordinates and existence of points change with AIRAC cycles.

Coordinates are WGS-84. Format is mixed DMS / decimal for readability. Where only approximate or partial data was available it is noted.

## 3. Santa Maria OAC (LPPO) Notes

Named 5LNCs are published in the Portuguese AIP (ENR 4.4 and ENR 6 charts). Boundary points along the eastern and southern FIR limits (approx. 45°N 013°W to 22°18'N 040°W) are preferred for entry/exit.  

Specific named points change; always consult the current AIP Portugal for the authoritative list and coordinates. Temporary points (e.g. ZUPAZ in recent supplements) also appear.
